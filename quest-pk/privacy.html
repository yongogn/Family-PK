Kinquest privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Kinquest is made for families. We don't collect, sell or share personal information, and there are no ads, no tracking and no accounts.

What stays on your device

Player names, ages, school years, avatars, progress, stars, badges, wishes and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive them. You can save a copy or delete everything from the app (💾 Save / restore).

Online play (optional)

Two-device games and far-away Boss PK connect two devices directly. To find each other, the devices use a small matchmaking (signalling) server and standard connection helpers. Only a temporary room code, the players' first names and the game moves are exchanged, and only between the two devices. Nothing is stored. A grown-up must allow online play first, and can turn it off in Grown-ups' corner.

Microphone

Speaking practice uses the microphone only while the child taps the 🎤 button. Speech checking uses the speech recognition built into the device's operating system. Recordings are not saved or sent to us.

Wishes to grown-ups

Wishes stay in the app's Wish Inbox. A grown-up can choose to send one to their own phone using the device's share sheet.

Purchases

Any optional purchase is handled by Apple and sits behind a grown-up check. We never see payment details.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, we have nothing to disclose to third parties. Parents can delete all data at any time by deleting the app or using 💾 Save / restore.

Contact

Questions? Email the developer at the support address on the App Store page.
