# iOS permission strings (add to ios/App/App/Info.plist after `npx cap add ios`)

```xml
<key>NSMicrophoneUsageDescription</key>
<string>Kinquest listens when you tap the microphone so it can check how you say a word. Your voice is never saved or shared.</string>
<key>NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription</key>
<string>Kinquest uses your iPad's speech recognition to check spoken words in spelling and Chinese practice.</string>
<key>NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription</key>
<string>Kinquest connects to another iPad nearby when you play a two-iPad game.</string>
```
